Frequently asked questions about documentation takeovers

How our exclusive sponsorships work alongside the broader EthicalAds network.

How are takeovers different from standard EthicalAds campaigns?

Standard EthicalAds campaigns are priced per impression (CPM) and rotate across hundreds of developer sites based on contextual topic matching. Documentation takeovers are dedicated, flat-rate sponsorships where you own 100% of the ad impressions on a single property.

How do booking and availability work?

Because each takeover is exclusive to one advertiser at a time, slots are reserved on a first-come, first-served basis for weekly or monthly runs. Select a property above to view details and request upcoming availability.

Can I run a takeover alongside regular network campaigns?

Yes. Many advertisers pair a documentation takeover with a broader Backend Web or network-wide campaign to combine prominent ownership on a flagship project with reach across hundreds of developer sites.

Can I request a takeover on another documentation site?

We are actively expanding takeovers to more of our best and top-performing publishers. Contact us to let us know which projects or technologies you want to reach, or see our publisher list for examples of sites on our network.
