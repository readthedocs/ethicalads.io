Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about booking the Flask documentation takeover.

How is this different from standard EthicalAds campaigns?

Standard EthicalAds campaigns are priced per impression (CPM) and rotate across hundreds of publisher sites based on contextual topic matching. This premium placement is a dedicated, flat-rate documentation takeover.

How does booking and scheduling work?

Because this is an exclusive placement, only a single advertiser can run at any given time. Placements are reserved on a first-come, first-served basis.

Can I update my creative or rotate copy during my run?

Yes. If you want to change your messaging, update a promotional URL, or test a new headline mid-campaign, just reach out to our support team and we will update your placement promptly.

Who can advertise on Flask's documentation?

Advertisers must offer products, tools, or services relevant to software developers and maintain the same quality standards as our core network. Both the EthicalAds team and the Flask maintainers review creatives for quality and community alignment.

Can I run this alongside regular EthicalAds network campaigns?

Absolutely. Many advertisers pair this dedicated property takeover with a broader Backend Web or network-wide campaign to achieve both targeted prominence on Flask and broad reach across hundreds of other developer documentation sites.

Can I book placements on other developer properties?

We are actively expanding dedicated premium placements across select high-traffic open source properties in our network. If you are interested in targeting other specific projects or technologies, contact us and let us know which properties you are interested in.
