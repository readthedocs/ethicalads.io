Frequently asked questions

How is this different from standard EthicalAds campaigns?

Standard EthicalAds campaigns are priced per impression (CPM) and rotate across hundreds of publisher sites. A takeover is a flat-rate month as the only sponsor across the Write the Docs website, newsletter, and Slack. Many advertisers pair it with a network-wide campaign for broader reach.

How does booking work?

There is one takeover and one newsletter sponsor each month, so months are first come, first served. Tell us the month you want, and we confirm availability with Write the Docs. Copy is due by the end of the month before.

Who can take over Write the Docs?

Sponsors must offer products, services, or jobs relevant to people who work on documentation. Both the EthicalAds team and Write the Docs review each takeover, and all copy has to follow the Write the Docs Code of Conduct.

Can I book placements on other developer properties?

Yes. We also offer takeovers on Flask and SQLAlchemy documentation. If there's another project you'd like to reach, let us know.
