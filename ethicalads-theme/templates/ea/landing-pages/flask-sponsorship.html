How is this different from regular EthicalAds campaigns?

Regular campaigns are priced per impression and rotate multiple advertisers through our standard placements. The premium sponsorship is a flat-rate monthly buy with 100% share of voice in a dedicated fixed header placement, plus logo placements that regular ads can't offer, like the Flask README. Flask's standard EthicalAds placement keeps running alongside it.

How much does it cost?

The Flask premium sponsorship is $1,000 per month, billed monthly with no long-term commitment. Only one sponsor holds the placement at a time. For broader reach, pair it with a campaign across our network of developer-focused sites.

Who can sponsor Flask?

Sponsors must be relevant to developers and meet the same quality standards as our ad network: no surveillance, no tracking, and no misleading creatives. The Pallets team has final approval over sponsors and creative.

Can I sponsor a different open source project?

We're building out premium sponsorships starting with Flask, and we plan to expand the program to more of the open source projects in our network. Get in touch and tell us which project you'd like to sponsor.
