Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about booking the Django banner sponsorship.

How is this different from standard EthicalAds campaigns?

Standard EthicalAds campaigns are priced per impression (CPM) and rotate across hundreds of publisher sites based on contextual topic matching. This premium placement is a dedicated, flat-rate banner sponsorship on the Django website and documentation.

How does booking and scheduling work?

Because this is an exclusive placement, only a single advertiser can run at any given time. Dates are reserved on a first-come, first-served basis, subject to confirmation by the Django Software Foundation.

Can I update my creative or rotate copy during my run?

Yes. If you want to change your messaging, update a promotional URL, or test a new headline mid-campaign, just reach out to our team and we will work with the Django Software Foundation to update your banner.

Who can sponsor the Django banner?

Advertisers must offer products, tools, or services relevant to software developers and maintain the same quality standards as our core network. Both the EthicalAds team and the Django Software Foundation review sponsorships for quality and relevance to Django developers.

Can I run this alongside regular EthicalAds network campaigns?

Absolutely. Many advertisers pair this dedicated property takeover with a broader Backend Web or network-wide campaign to achieve both targeted prominence on Django and broad reach across hundreds of other developer documentation sites.

Can I book placements on other developer properties?

Yes. We also offer dedicated premium placements on Flask and SQLAlchemy, and we are actively expanding to other high-traffic open source properties. If you are interested in targeting other specific projects or technologies, contact us and let us know which properties you are interested in.
