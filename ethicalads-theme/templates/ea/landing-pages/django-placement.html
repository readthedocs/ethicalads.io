Frequently asked questions

How is this different from standard EthicalAds campaigns?

Standard EthicalAds campaigns are priced per impression (CPM) and rotate across hundreds of publisher sites. This is a flat-rate, single-sponsor banner on Django's own site. Many advertisers pair it with a Backend Web or network-wide campaign for broader reach.

How does booking work?

Only one sponsor runs at a time, so dates are first come, first served. Tell us the week or month you want, and we confirm availability with the Django Software Foundation.

Who can sponsor the Django banner?

Sponsors must offer products, services, or opportunities relevant to software developers. Both the EthicalAds team and the Django Software Foundation review each sponsorship for quality and relevance to the Django community.

Can I book placements on other developer properties?

Yes. We also offer premium placements on Flask and SQLAlchemy. If there's another project you'd like to reach, let us know.
