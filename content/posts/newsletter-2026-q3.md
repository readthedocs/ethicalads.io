## New features from the previous 3 months

- Our updated [Q3 prospectus]({static}../prospectus/ethicalads-advertiser-prospectus.pdf) is out.
  There was a small [price increase]({filename}../pages/advertisers-pricing.md)
  for advertisers marketing to AI devs in North America.
- We are seeing great traction in the AI space.
  LLMs and chatbots are now our biggest source of new advertisers and publishers.
- Driven by a surge of inbound interest from publishers building AI tools and LLM integrations,
  we've released a [new landing page]({filename}../pages/landing-pages/ai-chatbots.md) dedicated to this space.
  This helps us better serve these newer products and the developers behind them
  and provides even richer contextual targeting for our advertisers.
- We've now showing metrics to publishers around how much of their site
  is indexed for [niche targeting]({filename}../pages/niche-targeting.md).
  The goal is to increase this number by making the data more transparent.
  Niche targeted campaigns yield the best results for advertisers in terms of performance
  and earn the most money for publishers: a win-win.

## Q2 advertising stats

[comment]: https://server.ethicalads.io/publisher/all/report/?start_date=2026-04-01&end_date=2026-06-30

Over the last quarter:

- We generated **$117.4k** for our publishers.
  While this is down slightly quarter-over-quarter as a few large publishers successfully transitioned to direct ad sales, our core network continues to thrive.
- We supported **208 active publishers** on our network.
  We welcomed quite a few new publishers to EthicalAds in the last quarter,
  including [mvnrepository.com](https://mvnrepository.com/), a site for Java Maven artifacts, and [mcpservers.org](https://mcpservers.org/), a collection of Model Context Protocol (AI tooling and data sources).
- We served **61.4M** paid ad views globally.
  While we saw a brief dip early in the quarter for broadly targeted campaigns,
  our network is now almost completely sold out across every market.

Demand in North America has been so strong we've had to delay advertiser start dates.
It's been the case for the last year or so that we've been limited by the number
of high-quality publishers we can onboard.
If you operate a site looking to monetize your audience of developers,
we'd love to [hear from you]({filename}../pages/publishers.md).


## Upcoming features

The major features in our upcoming roadmap for the next few months:

- As part of our [niche targeting]({filename}../pages/niche-targeting.md),
  we are enhancing our topic classifier for our [major audiences]({filename}../pages/advertisers.md#audiences).
  The goal is to take what we've learned in targeting niche campaigns
  and make even broadly focused campaigns perform better through improved targeting.
  Specifically, this is building on our work with [domain centroids]({filename}../posts/2025-niche-targeting-updates.md) and applying them to topics.
- We are actively building the infrastructure to serve ads directly from the "edge,"
  without needing to connect to a central server.
  While it may not launch this quarter, this will result in dramatically faster ad performance, especially for users further from our
  servers (mostly in US East).
- We're beginning a pilot with a large open-source project.
  This won't exactly be "advertising" but we're helping them get more sponsorships,
  including with marketing the benefits to sponsors and highlighting what sponsors receive.
  Helping out open-source projects has always been part of our DNA.
  There are many projects that don't want "ads" exactly but want sponsors,
  and we think this is an area where we can help.
