Today, we're introducing something new:
our first documentation takeover, starting with [Flask's official documentation]({filename}../pages/landing-pages/flask-placement.md).
Flask needs little introduction as one of Python's top web frameworks used by about a third of Python developers <sup>[1](https://lp.jetbrains.com/python-developers-survey-2024/#frameworks-and-libraries)</sup>,
and its docs get over 150k human visitors per month (more if you count agents, which we don't).
This new ad format gives developer marketers a great way to get their message in front of Python engineers right as they're building, while helping support an important open-source project.
You can check out the full details, or book a campaign, at our [Flask takeover page]({filename}../pages/landing-pages/flask-placement.md).


## How this is different (yet familiar)

There's a couple of differences between this new offering and our traditional ad network, but some things are the same:

- Rather than rotating ads on different pages, advertisers will be **the only** advertiser on Flask's documentation all week or all month long. This will be 100% share-of-voice with no other ads on the page whatsoever.
- The ad spot will be a prominent and always above-the-fold header placement at the top of the docs. Advertisers can visit Flask's docs during their campaign and see exactly what everyone else is seeing.
- For advertisers, this placement can be booked on its own or in addition to a traditional campaign on our network.
  It will give the same transparency and [reporting]({filename}../pages/advertiser-reporting.md) as the rest of our network.
