## What's included

A premium Django sponsorship is a single monthly buy that covers the most visible
real estate in the Django ecosystem:

- **A premium fixed header placement.**
  Your brand in a fixed header across djangoproject.com — including the documentation
  pages where Django developers spend their working day. Not an ad slot in a rotation:
  a placement reserved for premium sponsors.
- **Your logo in the Django README.**
  In front of every developer and every team evaluating Django on GitHub.
- **Front page sponsor recognition.**
  Your logo and a link in the sponsorship section of djangoproject.com's front page.
- **Direct support for Django itself.**
  Sponsorship revenue funds the Django Software Foundation and the paid Fellows who
  triage, review, and ship Django — support your customers and candidates can see.
- **A dedicated campaign manager.**
  Creative review before launch, [help crafting a message that converts](/advertisers/help-with-your-ads/),
  and proactive check-ins on your results.
- **Radically transparent reporting.**
  [Real-time performance](/advertisers/reporting/) for your placements — views, clicks, and
  geography — the same numbers we see.

Premium sponsorships are an add-on to the EthicalAds network:
pair yours with a [standard campaign](/advertisers/) to extend the same message across
Django and Python content on 200+ hand-approved developer sites,
with [AI-powered niche targeting](/advertisers/niche-targeting/).

## Why sponsorship beats traditional developer advertising

- **You can't retarget your way to developers.**
  Django developers block trackers and distrust surveillance advertising.
  A sponsorship placement is part of the site itself, on pages developers trust.
- **Context beats interruption.**
  Your brand appears while developers are actively building —
  reading the docs, evaluating the framework, shipping code — not while they scroll a feed.
- **Goodwill is a marketing channel.**
  Django shops notice who funds the framework they build on,
  and remember it when it's time to buy tools — or choose an employer.
- **Sponsors come back.**
  Renewals make up roughly 65% of EthicalAds' business. Advertisers who try us, stay.
