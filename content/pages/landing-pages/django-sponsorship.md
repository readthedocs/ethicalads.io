## Why sponsorship beats traditional developer advertising

- **You can't retarget your way to developers.**
  Django developers block trackers and distrust surveillance advertising.
  A sponsorship placement is part of the site itself, on pages developers trust.
- **Context beats interruption.**
  Your brand appears while developers are actively building —
  reading the docs, evaluating the framework, shipping code — not while they scroll a feed.
- **Goodwill is a marketing channel.**
  Django shops notice who funds the framework they build on,
  and remember it when it's time to buy tools — or choose an employer.
