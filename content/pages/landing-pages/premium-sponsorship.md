Developers are one of the hardest audiences in marketing.
They run ad blockers, skip sponsored search results,
and tune out anything that feels like it's tracking them.

A **premium sponsorship** puts your brand where developers actually spend their day:
inside the documentation, open source projects, and tools they depend on.
You get the scale and reporting of an ad network combined with the goodwill of a sponsorship,
because [70% of what you spend goes directly to the publishers and open source maintainers](/publisher-policy/) showing your message.
It's the same model behind the sponsorship programs of foundations like the
[Python Software Foundation]({filename}/posts/psf-ethicalads-partnership.md) — whose
sponsor recognition we power across python.org and PyPI — applied to an entire network of developer sites.

## Why sponsorship beats traditional developer advertising

- **You can't retarget your way to developers.**
  Developers block trackers and distrust surveillance advertising.
  Our ads are approved by [Acceptable Ads](https://acceptableads.com/standard/) and shown on sites developers trust,
  so your brand reaches people other networks never see.
- **Context beats interruption.**
  An ad next to the documentation a developer is using *right now* lands differently
  than one interrupting their social feed.
  Contextual targeting delivers high intent without touching personal data.
- **Goodwill is a marketing channel.**
  70% of your sponsorship goes directly to the maintainers and publishers displaying it.
  Developers notice who funds the tools they rely on — and remember it when it's time to buy.
- **Sponsors come back.**
  Renewals make up roughly 65% of our business. Advertisers who try us, stay.
